# Project: Autonomous Foundry & Coastal Habitat Prototype
## Objective: Low-Cost Resource Allocation & Hardware Deployment

### The Proposition
We are seeking a strategic resource partner to deploy a self-sustaining, minimal-footprint workshop and living prototype. This is not a traditional real estate build; it is an active R&D hardware deployment requiring minimal capital expenditure compared to standard industrial setups.

### What Is Required from the Partner
* **Hardware & Tech Provision:** Allocation of existing modular tech, prototyping units, or micro-processors.
* **Logistics & Relocation:** Support in transitioning core design assets and equipment to a secured operational site.

### What the Partner Gains
* **Direct R&D Integration:** First-access rights to the operational output of the Foundry workshop and automated core models.
* **Low-Cost, High-Impact Yield:** Maximum technological return for minimal capital reallocation.
* **Ownership / Licensing:** Clear structural stakes in the hardware frameworks and automated systems developed on-site.
